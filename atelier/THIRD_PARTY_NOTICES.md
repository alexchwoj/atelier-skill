Data files in `library/` derived from third-party work, used under their licences.

## Brand design systems (`library/designs/`)

Design-system descriptions of public brand websites, from the VoltAgent awesome-design-md collection. MIT License, Copyright (c) 2026 VoltAgent. The brand names and marks they describe belong to their owners; the files are references for inspiration, not licences to use any brand's identity.

## Font shape metrics (`library/fonts.json`)

Per-family measurements of Google Fonts typefaces (width, x-height, stem weight, stroke contrast and related traits), derived from a metric index published under the Apache License 2.0. The fonts themselves are not included; each is served by Google Fonts under its own licence (mostly the SIL Open Font License).

## Photographs (`library/photos.json`)

Links to photographs hosted on Wikimedia Commons, each under CC0, public domain, CC BY or CC BY-SA. Every entry records its author, licence and source page; credit the author and licence wherever a photo is used.
