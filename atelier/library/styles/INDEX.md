## Skeuomorphism

- [overview](skeuomorphism/index.md): The word predates screens: it is a 19th-century term for objects that imitate the materials of their ancestors, like pottery molded with fake metal rivets (say it "SKYOO-oh-morf-iz-um", from Greek skeuos, container or tool, plus morphe, shape).
