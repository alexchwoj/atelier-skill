# Hero Overlap Thin Bridge Strip

> Creates a full-window hero for Impact Minds with a very thin floating overlap strip that replaces the current strip below the hero. The strip bridges the hero and the next section and includes a premium insurance CTA.


---

SKILL NAME:
Hero Overlap Thin Bridge Strip

SHORT DESCRIPTION:
Creates a full-window hero for Impact Minds with a very thin floating overlap strip that replaces the current strip below the hero. The strip bridges the hero and the next section and includes a premium insurance CTA.

PROMPT:
Create a premium hero layout system for Impact Minds that uses a very thin floating overlap strip in place of the current strip below the hero.

This skill is for REPLACING the existing strip only.

Do NOT create a new block.
Do NOT add a second container.
Do NOT build a new section.
Do NOT duplicate the current strip.
Only replace the current strip with a refined overlap bridge strip.

HERO RULES
- Hero image or hero animation must fill the full visible browser window
- Hero must feel full-screen and cinematic
- Hero must NOT exceed visible window parameters
- Hero height must stay locked to the viewport so nothing important is pushed below the fold
- All core hero content must remain visible inside the viewport
- Maintain contained content width inside the hero
- Use a cinematic premium image with a soft overlay for readability
- Keep the hero calm, minimal, editorial, and premium
- Do not let the hero become taller than the screen
- Do not let hero content extend outside the first visible screen

HERO CONTENT
- Page label at top left above hero content
- Headline
- Subheadline
- CTA buttons
- Micro reassurance line

HEADLINE TYPOGRAPHY
- First half of headline: Roboto Bold
- Second half of headline: Roboto Italic
- Split across 2 lines
- Clear visual hierarchy between the two lines
- First line should feel like the anchor
- Second line should feel like the softer editorial continuation

OVERLAP STRIP SYSTEM
- Replace the current strip below the hero with a single thin floating overlap strip
- The strip must overlap the bottom of the hero image and the top of the next section
- 50% of the strip should cover the hero image
- 50% of the strip should sit inside the next section below
- The strip should act as a bridge between the hero and the next block
- Do NOT place the strip fully inside the hero
- Do NOT place the strip fully below the hero
- Do NOT create extra stacked content
- Do NOT turn it into a large card
- It must remain as thin as possible

SIZE AND PLACEMENT
- Total strip height must be 80px
- Keep the strip horizontally centered inside the page container
- The strip should not be full width
- Width should feel premium and intentional, approximately 70% to 85% of the main content container
- Add enough spacing below hero content so the strip does not collide with hero text or buttons
- The strip should feel like a light editorial bridge, not a bulky CTA block

CONTENT RULES
- All strip content and the CTA button must fit on a SINGLE LINE
- No stacked text
- No wrapped text
- No second row
- No section label needed
- No card subtitle above the content
- Keep copy minimal, clear, and premium
- Include one short support line on the left
- Include one single CTA button on the right

CTA RULE
- Use only ONE CTA button in the strip
- CTA should be insurance-focused
- Button text: Verify Insurance
- Make the insurance CTA feel premium, highly legible, and visually clear
- CTA should feel calm and confidence-building, not aggressive

LAYOUT
- Horizontal layout only
- Text aligned on the left
- Single CTA aligned on the right
- Everything vertically centered
- Maintain clean spacing between the text and the button
- Keep the strip elegant, slim, and visually quiet
- Avoid crowding

VISUAL STYLE
- Premium floating strip feel
- Rounded corners: 20px–24px
- Soft elevation
- Subtle shadow
- Light neutral background
- Cohesive with the hero image and next section
- No clutter
- No heavy borders
- No dark or harsh framing
- No black text
- Text colors: #7F776F or #2A2A2A only
- Primary CTA button color: #7F776F
- CTA text must remain high contrast and legible
- The strip should feel soft, editorial, and premium

MOTION
- Hero image should have subtle motion such as slow parallax or slow zoom
- The overlap strip can fade in softly
- The strip may have slight upward motion on reveal
- The CTA button may have a subtle hover lift
- Motion must be minimal, refined, and calm
- No flashy animation
- No bounce effects

SPACING AND STRUCTURE
- Maintain generous page margins
- Keep hero content inside the main content container
- Make sure the overlap strip does not block key hero content
- Keep the transition from hero to next section seamless and layered
- Ensure the strip feels integrated into the layout, not pasted on top of it

FINAL GOAL
- The result should feel like a premium editorial hero system with a very thin overlap bridge strip
- The strip should replace the current strip, not add a new one
- The strip should create depth without bulk
- The strip should improve conversion with a calm premium insurance CTA
- The overall effect should feel clean, structured, minimal, and intentional
