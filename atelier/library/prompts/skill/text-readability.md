# Text Readability

> This makes text legibility and readability a priority.


---

Prioritize text readability and accessibility across all sections of the page.

When text appears over images, videos, or complex backgrounds, ensure strong contrast and legibility using one of the following methods:

1. Add a soft overlay panel behind the text (preferred method).
   - Use a subtle translucent background layer or card.
   - Background should be neutral (white, off-white, or light grey).
   - Opacity range: 70–90%.
   - Include soft padding and rounded corners when appropriate.
   - Apply a subtle drop shadow for depth and separation.

2. Use a gradient fade overlay behind text if the design requires text directly on imagery.
   - Gradient should fade from neutral light color to transparent.
   - Ensure text sits in the highest-contrast portion of the image.

3. Adjust font color when necessary to maintain contrast.
   - Use dark text on light backgrounds.
   - Use white or light text on darker image areas.
   - Maintain WCAG-accessible contrast levels whenever possible.

4. Avoid modifying the original hue, color temperature, or visual tone of photos whenever possible.
   - Do not heavily darken or recolor images.
   - Preserve the natural warmth and tone of photography.

5. Maintain generous spacing around text to improve clarity and scanning.
   - Ensure headlines and body text are not placed directly on busy visual elements.

6. If readability cannot be achieved through font color alone, automatically introduce a background overlay or text container.

Design goal: maintain premium visual aesthetics while ensuring all text remains immediately readable on every screen size.
