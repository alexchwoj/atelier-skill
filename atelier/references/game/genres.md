# Genre patterns

Conventions players bring with them. Follow them for tier 1 information; spend originality on art direction, the signature moment and the screens players visit for pleasure (inventory, codex, results).

| Genre | HUD | Key screens | Watch |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **FPS / tactical shooter** | health bottom-left, ammo bottom-right, crosshair, hit markers, damage direction, compass or minimap, kill feed | loadout, scoreboard (hold to view), match results, settings with sensitivity | crosshair customisation, low visual noise, instant prompts; competitive players turn juice down |
| **Third-person action / soulslike** | health, stamina and focus top-left as long bars, quick slots bottom-left, lock-on reticle, boss bar | equipment with stat comparison, level-up with previews, map | the death screen is a ritual; readability of the boss bar and stamina is critical |
| **Action RPG / looter** | orbs or bars around a hotbar bottom-centre, minimap top-right, buff row | inventory with rarity and comparison, stash, crafting, skill tree, paragon boards | loot is the reward: rarity colour plus shape, beams on drops, fast comparison, sort and auto-sort |
| **MMO** | party frames, target and target-of-target, cast bars, quest tracker, chat | social, guild, auction house, mail, group finder | density and customisation (movable, scalable frames, addon-friendly) |
| **Strategy / RTS / 4X** | resource bar top, minimap bottom-left, selection panel and command card bottom, alerts right | tech tree, diplomacy, city or production screens, end-turn summary | information density with hierarchy; hotkeys everywhere; tooltips with numbers; "next unit/turn" flows |
| **MOBA** | own champion bottom-centre with abilities and items, team frames, minimap bottom-right, scoreboard on hold | item shop with builds, champion select, match results | ability cooldown legibility at a glance; ping system |
| **Roguelike / survivors** | health and XP, run timer, ability icons with levels, currency | upgrade choice (3 cards, fast, with synergies highlighted), run summary, meta progression | choices must be readable in under 2 seconds; run-over screen shows the build and what killed you |
| **Platformer** | lives, collectibles, timer top-left, often hidden | level select or world map, collectible tracker, results with time and rank | minimal HUD; very fast restart |
| **Racing** | speed and gear bottom-right, position and lap top-left, track map, delta and lap times | garage and tuning, career map, results with times | readable at speed; colour-coded deltas with +/- signs |
| **Rally and rallycross** | rally: stage time, split delta, progress line, pace-note calls, rev lights, gear and speed; rallycross: position, gaps, lap, joker lap state | stage select with surface and weather, car setup, service area repairs, results with split times | sim register: complete and precise, weighty condensed numerals, plates for dense data; pace notes readable in a glance |
| **Fighting** | health bars top with round timer, super meters bottom, combo counter, input display in training | character select (grid with art), training mode, frame data | symmetric layout; instant readability of meters |
| **Card / deckbuilder** | hand at the bottom, energy, draw and discard piles, enemy intents | deck builder, collection with filters, card upgrade, map | card text legible at the smallest size; keyword tooltips; play preview showing the result |
| **Puzzle** | moves or timer, score, hint | level select with stars, results | calm, minimal, no clutter over the board |
| **Horror** | almost none; diegetic health, inventory in-world or limited | save room menus, documents | fear needs absence: show only on demand |
| **Survival / crafting** | health, hunger, thirst, temperature as small icons, hotbar | crafting with recipes and have/need, building menu, base storage | many systems: progressive disclosure, track a recipe on the HUD |
| **Sim / management / tycoon** | money, time controls, alerts | overlays (data layers on the world), budget, graphs | data visualisation quality; tooltips with breakdowns |
| **Idle / incremental / mobile casual** | currencies top, big primary action, upgrade buttons | shop, events, daily rewards | big numbers formatting (1.2K, 3.4M, 5.6B), offline gains summary, avoid dark patterns |
| **VR** | diegetic first: wrist menus, holsters, objects | in-world panels 1 to 3m away | comfort settings; never HUD locked to the head except tiny, essential info |

**Big numbers:** use suffixes (K, M, B, T, then aa, ab… or scientific notation as an option), fixed decimals (1.25M), and tabular figures so they do not jitter.
