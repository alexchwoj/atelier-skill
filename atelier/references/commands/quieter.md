# quieter

Reduce intensity in a design that is too loud, busy or aggressive, without losing its personality or turning generic. Quiet design is harder than bold design: subtlety needs precision.

**By mode:** on Sell and Show pages, quieter means a more restrained palette, more whitespace and typographic air; the drama shrinks, the point of view stays. On Work and Read screens, it means less visual noise: fewer background accents, flatter containers, less colour and motion, so the tool disappears into the task.

## Find the sources

- **Colour:** too saturated, too many hues, accent everywhere.
- **Contrast:** extremes juxtaposed everywhere instead of where it matters.
- **Weight:** too many bold, heavy or large elements competing.
- **Motion:** too much, too long, too far.
- **Complexity:** decorations, patterns, borders, shadows, glows without a job.
- **Scale:** everything big, so nothing is.

Keep what works and the core message. If the purpose or audience is unclear, ask.

## Turn it down

- **Colour:** saturation down to 70 to 85% of the original chroma; fewer hues; neutrals carry more (around a 90/10 split); tinted greys instead of flat grey; never grey text on colour (use a darker shade of that colour).
- **Weight:** heavy weights down a step (900 to 700, 700 to 500); hierarchy from size, weight and space rather than colour and boldness; thinner or fewer borders.
- **Decoration:** remove gradients, glows, patterns and textures that do no work; simplify radii extremes; flatten layering.
- **Motion:** shorter distances (8 to 16px instead of 40), gentler easing, no bounce; remove animations without a clear purpose.
- **Composition:** smaller jumps between sizes; bring rogue elements back to the grid; even out spacing extremes.

## From the library

Restraint is learnable from the prompts that do it well: `find.mjs "<the page's kind> minimal editorial" --type prompt` and `parts.mjs <section type> --mood minimal --print 2`. Note how they carry hierarchy with scale, weight and space instead of colour and effects, and borrow that, inside the existing system.

## Do not

Make everything the same size or weight, remove all colour (quiet is not grey), remove all character, sacrifice affordances, or make everything small and light; a quiet page still needs anchors.

## Check

Still functional, still distinctive, easier to read for a long time, and the point of view survived the cuts. Generic quiet (grey text on white, one sans at one weight, a grid of identical cards) is a failure, not a success.
