# bolder

Raise a safe or flat part of a page to the conviction the rest of the design implies. Almost always scoped to something that exists: the surrounding page, its system and its conventions are the given.

The reflex answer (more effects, more gradients, more motion) is the opposite of bold. Reject it first.

## Scope is sovereign

Touch only the named target. Do not restyle its neighbours, move the page to a new idea, or add colours, fonts, radii, shadows or primitives the system does not already own. If the system genuinely cannot express what is needed, ask, naming the exact addition and the job it would do.

## Why it reads flat

A section usually reads flat for reasons its neighbours already solved. Compare: the display type at full strength, the structural devices that carry meaning, the signature motif, the density and pace. A flat section quietly opts out of the system's strongest moves.

## The amplification

- **Amplify what the system owns.** Its motif and its type scale at full strength, turned up for this section, not invented for it. The bolder version looks *more* like the same brand.
- **One decisive move, completely.** Scale (a head at display size, an image edge to edge), colour (a Field band where the page was all paper), structure (a pinned sequence, a list set huge), or contrast of density (one dense passage among quiet ones). Then quiet everything around it so the move is legible. If every element got louder, the section got flatter.
- **Give it its own rhythm.** The target should read as a peak in the scroll: a shift in density or pace, not more of the same.
- **Keep the content true.** Existing claims stay unless the user supplies new ones; if real evidence is essential to the bolder version and missing, ask for it.

## From the library

Before choosing the move, see how the best prompts make this kind of section a peak: `node scripts/parts.mjs <section type> --mood <the page's mood> --print 3`, and `find.mjs "<section> <mood> bold" --type prompt` for whole examples. Take the structural move (the scale, the bleed, the pinned sequence, the numbered list set huge), never its colours or faces; express it in the system's own tokens.

## The skeleton test

Strip the copy from the planned section and look at the bare structure. Does it still say what the section is and why it matters through hierarchy and the system's devices alone? If it only works once the words return, the boldness is in the font size, not the design. An image slot names a job (an anchor, a piece of evidence), never "a decorative photo here".

## Before you finish

- Everything outside the target is unchanged.
- No new colour, font or primitive appeared unasked.
- Actions in the section still work and still read as actions.
- It is unmistakably the same brand, only more sure of itself.
