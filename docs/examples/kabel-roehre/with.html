From hum to a clean saw

SignalHum only Pitchnone

  1. Listen first

    Every instrument is powered up slowly and played through before a screw comes out. We note what it does, not what the listing said.

  2. Recap and clean

    Dried capacitors come out. Pots, sliders, key contacts and jacks are cleaned and reseated.

  3. Calibrate

    Oscillators are trimmed to track across the keyboard; filters and envelopes go back to the service-manual figures.

  4. Burn in, then sell

    It runs on the bench before it is listed, then leaves with a year’s warranty.

One bench, four makers

We only work on Moog, Roland, Korg and ARP. Narrow on purpose: the same circuits cross the bench every week, so faults get found, not guessed.

12 months

Every instrument we sell carries a year’s warranty. If it drifts, crackles or goes quiet, it comes back to the bench.

Your own synth
