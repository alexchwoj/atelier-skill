01 — Concept

One vessel, two sails.

Vela means "sail." The mark is a small boat under steady wind: a mainsail for what you keep and a smaller jib for what you set aside. Both move the same boat forward — no alarms, no charts going up and to the right.

Mainsail — what's yours

The larger, taller sail. Deep harbor green, the color of still, deep water. It always carries most of the weight.

Jib — what's set aside

Lower and lighter, in warm ochre. It reads roughly a third of the composition — the share most freelancers reserve for tax.

The gap — separation

The open channel between sails is the product itself: two pots, clearly divided, never mixed up.

Hull — steadiness

A single soft curve grounds the mark. It's a horizon as much as a boat: calm water, no rush.

02 — On dark

Night watch.

05 — Typography

A soft serif, a plain sans.

Fraunces with its "soft" axis turned up gives the wordmark rounded terminals — friendly, bookish, unhurried. Instrument Sans handles everything functional with tabular figures for amounts.
