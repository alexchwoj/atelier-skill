One line, walked once

The mark is a single route. It starts on the baseline, climbs the first stem, crosses down to the valley and climbs again. It ends on the summit marker, the one point on the map you set before you leave. The stroke matches the weight of the letters, so the mark and the wordmark read as one hand.

Blaze, snowfield and night

Trail blazes are painted on trees so you can find the way in any light. The brand colour is that paint; the ink is the sky before a dawn start.

On blaze #E8592A, the whole lockup in ink.

On snowfield #F2F4F1 and white.

On night #0E1F2B, the name in snowfield.

4.2 seconds at walking pace

Timed at 100 beats a minute, a steady uphill step. The summit appears first, because a route app starts from where you want to go. The last second is stillness, so the finished mark is the thing you remember.
