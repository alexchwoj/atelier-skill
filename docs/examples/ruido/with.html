Ruido

Three days of electronic music inside an abandoned factory in Mexico City. The mark is the factory's sawtooth roof and a synth's sawtooth wave: one tooth for every day.

Dates and address: to be announced

Concrete by day, soot by night

The name is always soot or concrete. The teeth carry the red: bright red lead on dark grounds, deep red lead on light ones, so the mark never drops under 3:1.

The I in the name is cut like one more tooth, at the same angle as the mark, so name and mark read as one roofline.

Three teeth, any size

Solid triangles with wide gaps, so the mark holds at 16 px in a browser tab and at arm's length on a wrist. Each day lights its own tooth.
