How we cook

El fuego

The grill is lit at ten every morning and never touched by gas. Our asador builds the fire in one corner, then rakes the embers under the iron as each cut needs them — more heat for a crust, less for a long, slow render.

10:00

The wood

Quebracho and holm oak, burned down for two hours until only glowing embers remain.

800°at the core of the fire
13:00

The iron

Embers raked under a V-grooved grill that channels fat away from the flame. No flare-ups, no soot.

300°at the grill
Each order

The cut

Salt only — coarse, just before it hits the iron. Turned once. Never pressed.

54°inside, medium rare
Before serving

The rest

Every piece rests on warm wood for as long as it cooked, so the juices stay in the meat.

8′average rest
