The asador reads the fire by hand

No thermometer. A palm held over the iron, and a count until the heat says move. Try it.

0s

  1. Strong, to 3 sentraña, provoleta
  2. Medium, 4 to 6 sojo de bife, tira, chorizo
  3. Slow, 7 s and morevacío, mollejas

Press and hold the button (or hold Space). Let go when your hand would.

  1. Wood, never gas

    Hardwood burns down in a basket at the side of the grill. The flames stay there; only what falls through goes under the meat.

  2. Embers raked under the iron

    The asador shovels coals across in a gradient: a thick bed under the fast cuts, a thin one under the slow. One grill, three fires.

  3. Rested, then cut at the bar

    Every cut comes off and waits before the knife. The bar sits close enough to the grill that you watch it happen and feel it on your face.
