The detail

One detail: the underside of the e's crossbar is the swell from the seal. The tide sits inside the name.

The wordmark is Fraunces Bold, lowercase, tracked minus 20, outlined so it never depends on a loaded font. Its soft, slightly wonky serifs keep it warm for children and parents; nothing in it is sharp.

Sand, sea and volcano

Taken from the island, not from the surf shop wall: the pale dunes, the deep Atlantic past the reef, the rust of the old volcanoes, the turquoise of the shallows where lessons start.

The logo is one colour. Atlantic on light grounds, Dune on dark ones, Oxide only for the sticker and signage.

Oxide is for shapes and big words. It holds 4.3:1 on Dune: enough for the seal and headlines, not for body text.
