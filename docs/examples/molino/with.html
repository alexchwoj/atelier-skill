Molino

A sourdough bakery in Valencia that mills its own flour. The o in the name is the millstone.

Master logo in one colour, cobalt on flour.

The markA stone, dressed in quarters
1 2 3

The o is the stone that grinds the flour.

Millers cut grooves into the face of a stone so it shears the grain instead of crushing it. The pattern is called the dress. Molino's mark is that face seen from above, simplified until it holds at sixteen pixels.

  1. The eye

    The hole where grain falls in. It keeps the disc reading as a stone, not a button.

  2. Four dressed quarters

    Each quarter carries parallel furrows set at right angles to the next, the way a quarter dress is cut.

  3. Three furrows, then two

    Three per quarter from 32px up. The small cut drops to two, with a wider eye, for favicons and stamps.

On a dark groundReversed, flour on night

The reversed logo is one colour, flour, never outlined. Over photographs it sits on a night scrim so the furrows stay open. The loaf is a stand-in photograph until Molino has its own.

Small sizesFavicon, app icon, seal

The seal, for bag stamps, labels and the shop window. MOLINO over the top, VALENCIA beneath, the stone in the middle. Holds down to 64px.

Favicon: the small cut of the stone in flour on a cobalt tile. This tab is wearing it.

App and social avatar: cobalt stone on flour.

Stamped in night on crust, the colour of a kraft flour sack. The seal works in any single ink the printer has.
