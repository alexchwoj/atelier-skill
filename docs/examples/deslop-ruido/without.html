01 — The idea

Interference,
not decoration

RUIDO means noise. The wordmark is a clean, brutal signal that gets cut by interference: one strip of the letters slips sideways in sodium orange, the way a bad cable or a 140 dB sound system shakes an image apart. It's loud on purpose.

A / Mass

Ultra-condensed, ultra-heavy

Archivo at 62% width and 900 weight. Tall letters pack together like steel shutters on a factory wall. They hold up at the size of a stage banner and a 2 cm wristband alike.

B / Slip

One displaced strip

A 34-unit strip shifts right and turns orange. A hairline shifts left. That's all the "glitch" there is. Restraint keeps it a logo rather than an effect.

C / Light

Sodium vapour orange

The color of old industrial street lamps in Mexico City at 4 a.m. It is the only accent. Everything else is soot, concrete and bone.
