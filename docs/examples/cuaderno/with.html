Which two valves close to produce S1?

The mitral and tricuspid valves (the atrioventricular valves), at the start of ventricular systole.

Exam modeQuestion 3 of 40

A 62-year-old man has a holosystolic murmur, loudest at the apex and radiating to the left axilla. Which lesion is most likely?

Mitral regurgitation. Holosystolic, apical, radiating to the axilla. Aortic stenosis is a crescendo-decrescendo murmur at the right upper sternal border; mitral stenosis is diastolic.
