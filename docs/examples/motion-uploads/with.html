Every upload reports back

Start a few uploads. Each one gets a notice in the corner that fills as it sends, says when it’s done, and holds on to a failure until you retry it.

Drop files here

Or pick them from your computer. In this demo nothing leaves your machine: the upload is simulated with your file’s real name and size.

Try each notice

Every button runs a real sequence, so you see the notice change state rather than arrive finished.

  • Sends in about two seconds, turns into “uploaded”, then leaves on its own.

  • Stays in progress for about twenty seconds; the ring and the count fill as it goes.

  • Drops partway, moves to the front and waits. Retry carries on from where it stopped.

  • Watch them stack. Hover, tap or tab into the stack to fan it out.

Esc folds the stack back up.
