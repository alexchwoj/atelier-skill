About

Barcelona, ES
Studio view, Gràcia

Inés Vidal is an architecture and landscape photographer working from a small studio in Gràcia, Barcelona.

Trained as an architect at ETSAB before turning to the camera full-time in 2016, she works with practices, developers and publishers who want images that describe a building honestly: its materials, its weather, the way it meets the ground.

Alongside commissions she pursues long-form landscape series across Catalonia and the Pyrenees, shot on large-format film and digital. Her work has been exhibited in Barcelona, Madrid and Porto, and she teaches a yearly workshop on photographing space.
