How a commission runs

  1. Brief

    You send drawings, the shot list you need and where the pictures will be used.

  2. Site visit

    I walk the building first to find which façade gets which light, and when.

  3. Shoot

    Booked on the right day and hour, with a fallback date if the weather turns.

  4. Delivery

    Corrected, retouched files for print and web, licensed for your use.
