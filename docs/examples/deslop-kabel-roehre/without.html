01 / How we restore

Five stages. No shortcuts in the signal path.

Every instrument goes through the same chain, whether it’s a €400 SH-101 or a Jupiter-8. We log each step — measurements, replaced parts, before-and-after scope captures — and the logbook ships with the synth.

In · Stage 1

Intake & diagnosis

We power it up on an isolation transformer, listen, measure and photograph everything before a single screw turns.

  • Rail voltages & ripple
  • Oscillator drift test
  • Written estimate
~ 2 hrs
Stage 2

Teardown & deep clean

Boards out, keybed out. Every pot and slider is cleaned and lubricated, not just sprayed. Cases are washed by hand.

  • Pots & sliders serviced
  • Contacts re-tinned
  • Wood cheeks oiled
~ 6 hrs
Stage 3

Component work

Electrolytics recapped, tired trimmers replaced, keybed bushings renewed. Original ICs kept wherever they still sing.

  • Full recap (where due)
  • Battery & leak repair
  • Original parts bagged
~ 8 hrs
Stage 4

Calibration

Factory service manual on the bench, frequency counter in hand. 1V/oct scaling across eight octaves, filter tracking, envelopes.

  • Scaling to ±0.5 cent
  • Filter & VCA trim
  • Voice matching (poly)
~ 4 hrs
Out · Stage 5

72-hour burn-in

It stays switched on for three days in a warm room, then we recheck tuning cold. If it drifts, it goes back to stage four.

  • Thermal cycling
  • Final play-test
  • Logbook signed
72 hrs

03 / Why Kabel & Röhre

Buying vintage shouldn’t feel like a gamble.

Garantieschein · WarrantyNº KR-26
12months

Parts, labour, and the drive across town.

If anything we touched fails within a year, we fix it — free, no arguing about what counts. Inside Berlin we’ll even collect it from your studio.

GeprüftK&R · Berlin

  1. Originality over “upgrades”.

    No mystery mods, no swapped filters. If a part has to go, the original is bagged, labelled and returned with the instrument.

  2. A logbook, not a promise.

    Every synth leaves with a printed restoration log: what we measured, what we replaced, and scope captures before and after.

  3. Specialists, not generalists.

    We only work on analog synthesizers. Four technicians with a combined 60 years at the bench — and a drawer of NOS parts most shops don’t have.

  4. Play before you pay.

    Every instrument is plugged in and waiting in the showroom. Book an hour, bring your headphones, take your time.
